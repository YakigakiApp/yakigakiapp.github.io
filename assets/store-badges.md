# Store badges

Black English badges matching the supplied visual reference. Keep the artwork's aspect ratio and use a minimum height of 40px.

- `app-store-badge.svg`: Apple distribution at https://developer.apple.com/assets/elements/badges/download-on-the-app-store.svg
- `google-play-badge.svg`: Google Play artwork mirrored as SVG at https://github.com/pioug/google-play-badges/blob/main/svg/en.svg (source noted by the repository: https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/).

App Store and Apple branding belong to Apple Inc. Google Play and its logo belong to Google LLC.
